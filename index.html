Philosophy

How we think.

On Process

"A structured search is not slower — it's the only one that works."

We define success criteria before we start sourcing. A scorecard, a set of must-haves, and alignment on what great looks like. No assumptions, no ambiguity. The search begins from a shared definition of the outcome.

On Relationships

"Access is built over years. It cannot be rushed or replicated."

Our network in private capital is the product of long relationships — not LinkedIn. We know who is open, who is excellent, and who would never take a call from anyone else. That access is what we bring to every mandate.

Engagement Process

Built for precision.

Four stages. No shortcuts. Senior attention at every step from intake through close.

I
Alignment

We define the scorecard, must-haves, and outcomes before sourcing begins. What does great look like? What are the non-negotiables? Alignment here is what makes the shortlist tight.

II
Market Map

Every relevant candidate in the market is identified before outreach begins. We map the talent universe, assess competitive compensation, and build an intelligent target list.

III
Shortlist

Structured assessment and 3–6 qualified candidates. High-signal only — we do not pad shortlists. Every name has been screened, profiled, and assessed for fit against the scorecard.

IV
Close

Offer structuring, candidate management, and reference support through to a signed offer. We stay fully engaged until the search is complete — not until the presentation is made.
